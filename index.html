简介About

周阳，音乐艺术博士（DMA），现任四川文理学院音乐与演艺学院副教授。先后就读于中央音乐学院（小提琴演奏学士）、伯克利波士顿音乐学院（小提琴演奏硕士）与亚利桑那大学（小提琴演奏博士，辅修音乐教育学，2024）。 Lance Zhou holds a Doctor of Musical Arts in Violin Performance with a minor in Music Education, and is currently Associate Professor at the School of Music and Performing Arts, Sichuan University of Arts and Science. He studied at the Central Conservatory of Music (BM, Violin Performance), Boston Conservatory at Berklee (MM, Violin Performance), and the University of Arizona (DMA, 2024).

他的工作横跨舞台与课堂：作为演奏者，曾任职于亚利桑那交响乐团及歌剧乐团并参与室内乐与音乐节演出；作为教师，主持小提琴演奏与乐队建设相关课程。研究兴趣集中在三个方向——小提琴教学法与教学曲目体系、室内乐教育的低门槛协作路径，以及人工智能在曲谱分析与教学曲库中的应用。 His work spans stage and classroom. As a performer he has played with the Arizona Symphony and Opera Orchestra and in chamber music and festival settings; as a teacher he leads courses in violin performance and school ensemble building. His research focuses on three areas: violin pedagogy and graded teaching repertoire, low-barrier collaborative pathways into chamber music, and applications of AI to score analysis and teaching libraries.

研究Research

小提琴教学法Violin Pedagogy

教学曲目的难度分级与递进路径Grading and progression in teaching repertoire

以 Mazas 小提琴二重奏体系为线索，分析其作品的教学结构，并建立调性、速度、左右手技术难度的多维评分框架，用于规划从入门到专业的学习轨迹。博士论文：Des Sons Harmoniques: Mazas's Legacy on Violin Pedagogy（2024）。 Using Mazas's violin duos as a thread, this work analyses their pedagogical structure and builds a multi-dimensional rating framework (key, tempo, left- and right-hand demands) for planning learning trajectories from beginner to advanced. DMA dissertation: Des Sons Harmoniques: Mazas's Legacy on Violin Pedagogy (2024).

室内乐教育Chamber Music Education

以熟悉曲目为入口的协作教学Collaborative teaching through familiar repertoire

研究"一人一声部、相互倾听、角色独立、协同对话"的室内乐学习机制，通过分层声部编配、角色轮换与即兴伴奏脚手架降低入门门槛：入口变了，目标没变。 A study of chamber music as a learning mechanism — one player per part, mutual listening, independent roles, collaborative dialogue — lowering the entry barrier through layered part-writing, rotating roles, and improvisational accompaniment scaffolds.

AI + 音乐教育AI & Music Education

曲谱智能分析与动态教学曲库Intelligent score analysis and dynamic teaching libraries

探索光学乐谱识别（OMR）、多模态曲谱分析与可计算的技术难度参数，把演奏难点转成可检索、可推荐的数据，服务于因材施教。 Exploring optical music recognition (OMR), multi-modal score analysis, and computable difficulty parameters, turning technical demands into searchable, recommendable data for adaptive teaching.
